## Main store listing

**Short description** (up to 80 characters):

```
Find your life partner in the Goswami community. Profiles, chat and calls.
```

**Full description:**

```
GoswamiJodi helps members of the Goswami community and their families find a life partner.

CREATE YOUR PROFILE
Add your photos, family details, education, career and partner preferences. Download your biodata as a PDF to share with family.

FIND MATCHES
Browse profiles from the community, search by member ID and see how well a profile fits your preferences. Send an interest to the people you like.

TALK SAFELY IN THE APP
Chat, send voice notes and make voice and video calls without sharing your mobile number.

YOU STAY IN CONTROL
Choose who can see your mobile number, hide your profile at any time, and block or report anyone who bothers you. Our team reviews every report.

PREMIUM MEMBERSHIP
Premium members can see mobile numbers, as each member's privacy settings allow, and message members on WhatsApp. Memberships aren't sold in this app.

FOR ADULTS, FOR MARRIAGE
GoswamiJodi is for people aged 18 or over who are looking for a marriage partner. It is not a dating service.

You can delete your account and your data at any time, in the app or at goswamijodi.com/delete-account.
```

### Data safety form

**Overview**

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Does the app collect or share user data? | Yes |
| Is all data encrypted in transit? | Yes |
| Can users ask for their data to be deleted? | Yes, in the app and at https://goswamijodi.com/delete-account |

**Data collected.** For every row, choose *Collected: Yes*, *Shared: No*, and *Required* or *Optional* as shown. "Shared: No" is correct because Google doesn't count service providers, or members showing their own profile to others, as sharing.

| Google's data type | What it is in the app | Required? | Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|
| Personal info: Name | Name on the profile | Required | App functionality, Account management |
| Personal info: Email address | Login | Required | Account management, Developer communications |
| Personal info: Phone number | Mobile number | Required | App functionality, Account management |
| Personal info: Address | City and state on the profile | Required | App functionality |
| Personal info: Race and ethnicity | Community and sub-community | Optional | App functionality |
| Personal info: Political or religious beliefs | Religion | Optional | App functionality |
| Personal info: Other info | Date of birth, gender, family, education, career, horoscope | Required | App functionality |
| Financial info: Purchase history | Plan, amount, payment reference | Optional | App functionality, Account management |
| Photos and videos: Photos | Profile photos | Optional | App functionality |
| Audio: Voice or sound recordings | Voice notes | Optional | App functionality |
| Messages: Other in-app messages | Chats | Optional | App functionality |
| App activity: Other actions | Interests, shortlists, profile visits, blocks, reports | Required | App functionality, Fraud prevention, security and compliance |
| Device or other IDs | Notification token (once notifications are added) | Required | App functionality |

**Not collected:** precise location, contacts, calendar, health, payment card or bank details, files, web history, advertising ID.

Calls are not recorded, so they aren't listed. The microphone and camera are used live and nothing is stored.
